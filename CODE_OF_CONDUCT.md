# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. Its full text applies
to everyone who takes part in this project, in every project space (issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Our pledge, in short

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an
open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community: we are kind, we accept
constructive feedback, we take responsibility for our mistakes, and we focus on what is
best for the community as a whole.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **dev@pokamind.com**. Every report is reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the person reporting
are respected. The maintainers apply the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) when deciding on the
consequences of a violation.
